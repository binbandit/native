Painted calavera artwork

Method: built-in imagegen, transparent foreground, followed by layered assembly and rendering in Apple Icon Composer.
Visual reference: https://owalalife.com/en-au/blogs/water-cooler/behind-the-design-sugar-skull
The reference was studied for colour, painting density and celebratory character. Its product, logo and artwork are not included in the icon.

Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: the isolated foreground artwork for a premium macOS app icon, square canvas. Create a NEW original traditional Mexican Día de Muertos calavera sugar skull, FULLY PAINTED over its entire face. Image 1 is a reference photograph from the user's inspiration page: use only its saturated folk-art palette, the density of painted decoration and celebratory mood as visual inspiration. Do not reproduce its water bottle, branded artwork, layout, scene or any product. The output must be one freestanding skull, and absolutely no bottle. Strong near-frontal symmetrical broad skull silhouette, rounded cranium, sculpted cheekbones, short gently rounded jaw. Three-dimensional crafted sugar/painted ceramic feel with subtle convincing volume and beautiful hand-applied paint, refined enough for a carefully art-directed native Mac application. A deep ink black or midnight plum shell, COVERED with vivid turquoise, hot coral pink, marigold yellow-orange, cobalt blue and warm ivory painted motifs. Large open dark floral eye sockets surrounded by broad colourful scalloped flower petals and nested fine dots. Painted floral scrollwork and teardrop motifs fill the forehead, temples, cheeks, nose bridge and chin. A small inverted-heart nose. A joyful gentle grin with TWO rows of individually formed warm ivory teeth, with hand-painted pink borders, no stitched horizontal line. Rich hand-painted symmetrical cheek flourishes and a large painted fan or flower on the chin. Ornament should be bold enough to read as a painted calavera at small icon sizes, with fine craftsmanship visible up close. Leave only a small unlettered dark cartouche high at the centre of the forehead, bordered by colourful painted curls, where the app's own tiny maker's mark can later be placed. Integrate marigold floral shapes into the PAINT, not a separate flower crown. Soft sculptural lighting, slight edge light to define the black silhouette. No photorealistic human bone, no horror, no creepy eyes, no emoji, no flat generic vector skull, no bare white face with a few decorations, no rhinestones, no excessive glitter or metallic jewellery. Composition: completely isolated on a truly transparent background, skull centred, whole skull visible with comfortable padding, occupies about 78 percent of canvas height and 66 percent width. No app icon backplate, no square container, no backdrop, no floral wreath outside the skull, no cast shadow outside the object, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark.

Native assembly

Keep the generated painted foreground unchanged. Place the exact Native interlock in the forehead cartouche as its own editable vector layer. Use Native's tile treatment and channel cues: warm ivory for Regular, amber for Beta, lavender and a conspicuous star field for Nightly, and cyan with the blueprint surface for Dev. Set the painted foreground scale to 66 percent in Icon Composer so it has clear margins. Export Default, Dark, ClearLight, ClearDark, TintedLight and TintedDark using Apple's renderer.
